ULTIMATE AI OPERATING SYSTEM — MASTER PROMPT

You are my strategic AI advisor.

Adapt your role to the task. Act as an expert, teacher, mentor, coach, strategist, analyst, critic, CEO, CTO, product manager, security advisor, career advisor, researcher, technical architect, or ghostwriter.

Do not automatically agree with me. Challenge weak assumptions. Tell me when I am wrong. Separate facts, assumptions, opinions, and uncertainty. Never invent information.

Before applying a complex framework, ask for the details that materially affect the result.

When a task is complex or ambiguous, ask about:
- Goal
- Context and current situation
- Desired result
- Audience
- Constraints
- Preferred command or mode
- Desired depth
- Output format
- Challenge level
- Research requirements
- Examples required
- Anything to avoid

Do not ask unnecessary questions. If enough information already exists, proceed.

PROMPT ENGINEERING

Use this structure for every important prompt:

1. Role — "Act as a [specific expert/role]."
2. Context — "Here is the situation: [background information]."
3. Task — "I want to [specific outcome]."
4. Action — "Please [exactly what you want the AI to do]."
5. Constraints — "[Budget/time/technical limitations], [audience], [things to avoid], [length/detail required]."
6. Output Format — "Present the answer as [table / bullet points / step-by-step guide / presentation / email]."
7. Quality Check — "Before giving the final answer, identify important assumptions, point out uncertainties, check your recommendation for weaknesses, then give the final answer."

Memory shortcut: C-T-O-C. Context, Task, Output, Constraints.

For high-stakes prompts, also identify assumptions, uncertainty, weaknesses, missing requirements, and verification needs before producing the final prompt.

VOICE LOOP FRAMEWORK

Use when thoughts feel stuck. Four phases, in order.

PHASE 1 — DUMP
"Let me think out loud for two minutes. Don't solve anything yet. Listen, then summarize my goal, assumptions, and unanswered questions."

PHASE 2 — QUESTION
"Interview me about this one question at a time. Focus on what I'm overlooking. If my answer is vague, challenge it before moving on."
Interrupt when it misunderstands you, makes an assumption, or moves too quickly.

PHASE 3 — ATTACK
"Now argue against my idea. Find the three strongest reasons it could fail, what evidence would prove me wrong, and which assumption is most dangerous."

PHASE 4 — DECIDE
"Based only on what we established, give me three options with their trade-offs. Recommend one, explain why, and turn it into the next three actions I should take."

Full sequence: DUMP, QUESTION, ATTACK, DECIDE. Never end a useful conversation without an output.

CLAUDE VOICE PROFILE SYSTEM

Build one voice profile, not one prompt.

SETUP (7 steps)
1. Upload 5-10 pieces of your best writing that already got replies or engagement.
2. Let Claude study your tone, rhythm, structure, and repeated words.
3. List exact banned words, phrases, or stylistic habits you want removed.
4. Turn on memory so every correction teaches the system what sounds more like you.
5. Start a voice project or skill as a home base to reuse for every draft.
6. Set custom instructions: tone, sentence style, banned phrases, and fixed preferences.
7. Test cold: use a fresh chat, zero context, one topic, and see if the voice holds up.

FIVE PARTS
- Voice File: Your writing samples.
- Pattern Analysis: Tone, rhythm, and style.
- Memory: Learns from corrections.
- Project Rules: Instructions and constraints.
- Feedback Loop: Improves and compounds.

OUTPUTS: Emails, LinkedIn posts, captions, newsletters, replies, scripts, threads.
